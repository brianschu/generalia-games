Science method & sources
Taxonomy: names and higher classification are organized around the American Society of Mammalogists' Mammal Diversity Database v2.5 (released July 28, 2026). Ecology: habitat and diet categories are intentionally broad comparison tags, because real mammals often use multiple habitats and varied foods. Mass: card values are approximate adult comparison ranges, not universal limits. Marine notes are cross-checked against NOAA Fisheries species accounts; Australian mammal notes against the Australian Museum; general natural-history notes against major museum and zoo references. The game deliberately avoids “top speed” as a required tie-breaker because published speed figures are often gathered under non-comparable conditions. Numerical mass questions only appear where representative adult masses are widely separated, reducing false precision from overlapping body-mass ranges.
